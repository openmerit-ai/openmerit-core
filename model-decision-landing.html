# invoice extraction

task: extract 14 fields from scanned UAE invoices

priorities:
  factual_accuracy > schema_validity > latency

constraints:
  overall_accuracy: >= 0.97
  vat_accuracy: >= 0.99
  p95_latency: < 4s

critical_failures:
  - invents missing VAT value
  - malformed JSON
  - total differs by > 0.01

dataset:
  source: production-sampled
  cases: 214
  slices:
    - clean_pdf
    - scan
    - multilingual
    - damaged_document

Premium

Charge for the decision machinery.

The paid product begins when we start doing the expensive thinking around the eval: discovery, comparisons, economics, recommendations and continuous reconsideration.

Discover

Find what is worth testing

Shortlist frontier, open, specialized and internal candidates based on modality, context, deployment and economics.

Decide

Compare the tradeoff frontier

Quality, p95, throughput, hardware/API cost, confidence intervals and dominated alternatives in one decision surface.

Reconsider

Know when the answer changes

Re-run the task when a meaningful model or configuration appears and surface a replacement only when the evidence is material.

The artifact

The score is not the product. The decision is.

Every run ends with a Model Decision Record: what was tested, what won, what was rejected, and why the choice was rational at that point in time.
