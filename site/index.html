A few useful details

Fair questions.

Is OpenMerit an agent?+

No. It’s an orchestration layer that works with your harness. The harness understands your project, builds evaluations, tests models, and applies authorized changes. OpenMerit defines the outcomes, tracks evidence, and checks that the process and returned results conform to your policy.

Which harnesses does it work with?+

Pi is the first implemented adapter. The protocol and core are harness-neutral, so another harness can connect through an adapter. There isn’t a second adapter yet.

Building a harness adapter ↗
Will it change my model without asking?+

During supervised graduation, a verified proposal waits for your explicit approval with /openmerit approve. Every swap requires verification. Later automation and rollback follow the policy you confirm; you can pause proactive nudges with /openmerit pause.

Where does my data live?+

OpenMerit keeps snapshots, an append-only audit log, and evidence manifests in your project’s .openmerit/ directory. Provider access and credentials belong to your harness. Evaluations may reference external evidence systems you use with that harness.

Read the security notes ↗
What does it cost to run?+

OpenMerit is open source and free to install from npm, with no hosted subscription. Your model provider’s usage charges still apply, including challenger trials. You confirm an evaluation budget during setup.
