OpenMerit

The frontier is moving faster than ever, and there are more great models to choose from than at any point in history. We love that. It also means the best model for your task last month might not be the best one today, and no public leaderboard is going to tell you which one it is. OpenMerit exists to make sure you get the most out of all that progress. We pick the best model for every task in your app, judged on your own evals, and keep updating that pick all day, every day if needed. Your harness changes, the catalogue keeps growing, and nobody (human or agent) should spend their life benchmarking models against every agent and task. Your product team should be building for customers, not speed-dating models.

Now, we know what you’re thinking: “Cool, so it’s a router.” It’s not a router. “Auto” routers guess per query, sit in your live path, and optimize for metrics that aren’t yours. Even if one could see your evals, it couldn’t run them mid-request, because your real quality bar lives in product evals written by you (or your agent) for your app, not in a millisecond guess. And let’s be honest, writing and running those evals is the bane of your existence. We get it. That’s why we plug into the eval setup you already have and do the heavy lifting for you. We don’t touch your calls, and we don’t sell you tokens (we checked, it’s a very crowded business).

Meet Scout, the part of OpenMerit that makes finding models easier for you. It’s in beta, which means it occasionally asks for directions. Help us shape it.
