    Privacy & analytics

    Help improve Scout during beta. We use enabled prompt, recommendation and usage logging to evaluate matching and fix problems, not to train a foundation model. You can turn off sharing below; existing opt-outs are preserved.

    Our service providers process data on our behalf: Cloudflare hosts Scout, OpenAI processes tasks, Clerk handles sign-in, and PostHog stores enabled analytics. Account analytics can include your name, verified email and sign-in provider information. No session recording.

    Unlinked activity uses separate request IDs, not your account identity. This does not erase earlier links or stop separate account-profile and sign-in analytics. Prompt text can still identify you.
