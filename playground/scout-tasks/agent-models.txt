We are building an AI-native health insurer's outpatient claims pipeline in Dubai (all test data is fictional). Five agents each need a model. They run as Pi Durable agents on Cloudflare Workers and call JSON-schema tools in multi-step loops.
1. Enrollment: read an application and a free-text health declaration, list declared chronic conditions as ICD-10-CM category prefixes, then record an eligibility decision via a tool.
2. Claims intake/extraction: read an eClaimLink-style claim (structured) plus a free-text itemized bill, match bill lines to claim activities, total them and list mismatches, then record via a tool.
3. Medical coding review: check ICD-10-CM and CPT codes with lookup tools, detect diagnosis/procedure mismatch, unbundling and E/M upcoding, record findings.
4. Coverage and adjudication: apply plan rules from tools, decide pay or deny per claim line with denial codes; amounts are computed by a tool.
5. Fraud, waste and abuse review: compare a claim with member history and provider statistics, score signals 0-1 and recommend pay, review or hold.
Constraints: UAE health-data residency. Under UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 Art. 13, health data must be stored and processed inside the UAE, so each model endpoint must process data in the UAE; say where each endpoint processes data, and treat unknown as outside the UAE. We already use Cloudflare Workers AI and prefer it. Low volume (hundreds of claims a day); quality and reliable tool calling matter more than cost.
